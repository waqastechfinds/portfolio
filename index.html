Sample SEO audit: Font Craft

A self-audit of my own tool site, with data from PageSpeed Insights, W3C, Rich Results Test and Google Search Console (October 2026).

Findings

  • Performance: 100 on mobile and desktop. LCP 1.0 s on slow 4G, 0.3 s on desktop. Layout shift 0.
  • Code: no W3C errors. Organization and Software Application structured data are valid.
  • Indexing: 10 pages indexed, 1 crawled but not yet indexed.
  • Visibility: early stage, 126 impressions and 5 clicks in three months, average position 12.5.

Recommendations

  • Fix the one Accessibility item to move from 97 to 100.
  • Improve the one page that is not indexed, then request indexing.
  • Add rating markup only when real reviews exist.
  • Earn relevant backlinks through editorial guest posts and publish how-to articles linked to each tool page.
